# Homepage surface brief

Scope: homepage (`src/App.jsx` and its sections). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: an Iranian parent at night on a phone, wanting tonight's story for a child, or an adult with a private health question. Action: make tonight's story right away, free, without signing up; sign up afterwards to save it. Proof on hand: the working story maker itself; health answers marked as awaiting doctor review; every testimonial or doctor name stays a labelled placeholder.

Constraint from the user: moving video backgrounds behind the sections, full-screen and cinematic. Videos are rendered by us from our own three.js korsi scene, muted, looping, with a visible pause control and a still poster under reduced motion.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is a korsi on a snowy winter night, the low quilted table where Iranian grandparents told stories; tonight's story is made right there on the quilt. It refuses the category's pastel mother-and-baby landing with a split hero and a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Ghalamkar and termeh quilt cloth as the material: madder red, indigo night, saffron, walnut wood, unbleached cotton only as thin piping, never as ground. Warm light pools from under the quilt; the window holds a cold blue moon and falling snow. Panels are quilt bands with a stitched edge and boteh repeat; buttons are saffron. Lalezar sets every headline, Vazirmatn the rest.

STORY: The visitor sees a lit korsi, reads that tonight's story can be made here in a minute, makes one with their child's name, reads it, then learns that health answers are reviewed and private, and signs up with a mobile number to keep the story.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed looping video of the korsi room at night. Lalezar headline on the right in RTL. The story maker sits on a quilt panel in the first viewport: child's name field, three hero chips, one saffron "قصه را بساز" button; the finished story unfolds in place on the same panel. Header is a thin dark band with nav and a quiet sign-in link.

FORM: Korsi winter night, position 4 of 7 on the grounded list, seed key 2e2d4173 (degraded roll, no challengers). Signature interaction: the story is made inline in the first viewport and unfolds on the quilt.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
